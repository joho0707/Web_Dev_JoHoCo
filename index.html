Car Of The Day

Chevrolet Corvette C5

By © Vyacheslav Argenberg, CC BY 4.0, Link

The Chevrolet Corvette C5 (1997-2004) marked a turning point for America's sports car. Built from a clean-sheet design, it replaced the long-running C4 with a stiffer, lighter, and far more refined platform, and it remains one of the best performance bargains on the used market today.
